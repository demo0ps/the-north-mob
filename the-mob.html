/// AGENDA 01 — THE MOVEMENT

The Movement

THE NORTHMOB treats the music like a startup — self-funded, self-promoted, self-executed. No label waiting to discover them.

The Sound

Trapcardi. US trap's 140 BPM grit — the 808s, the skittering hats — locked to Bacardi's rolling bounce, the sound Pretoria's taverns and street corners grew up on.

Street subject matter. Pitori dancefloor rhythm. A lane they built, not one they joined.

The Model

A fiercely independent collective — a self-contained unit that funds its own recordings, visuals and rollouts.

Family First: earnings, royalties and booking revenue are pooled and split equally. Everybody eats, or nobody does.

The Come-Up

Ga-Rankuwa cyphers and raw mixtapes → Cold Streets breaking out → camp, chair with Focalistic → the SAMA32 stage → debut mixtape HERE 4 BUSINESS (Aug 2026).

Platform borrowing, not platform selling. Independence intact.
